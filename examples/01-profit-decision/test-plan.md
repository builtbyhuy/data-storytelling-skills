# A four-week pilot that ends with an honest decision

**Status:** proposed. Roles, customer eligibility, prices and thresholds require the business owner's approval before execution. This example does not assume those approvals or staff availability.

**Question:** does a modest reduction in the B2B discount improve contribution without unacceptable lost business or service harm? The VND6.20m sensitivity is a scale check, not the target or expected result.

## The operating plan

| Week | Accountable role | Deliverable and decision |
|---|---|---|
| 1 — establish readiness | Finance owner + commercial lead; operations director approves | Reconcile the missing VND3m of July freight to the ledger. Define eligible comparable B2B accounts and exclusions for contracts. Predefine the approved price change, minimum worthwhile contribution gain, acceptable demand loss and complaint limits. Check that account-level contribution and a comparable holdout can actually be measured. If not, postpone the intervention and repair the evidence first. |
| 2 — open the bounded test | Commercial lead | Assign eligible accounts to test and concurrent holdout at account level, using a reproducible random allocation where feasible. Set a cap on affected accounts and exposure before launch. Hold products, delivery promises and other promotions stable where operationally possible; record exceptions. |
| 3 — close intake | Commercial lead + customer-service owner | Apply the agreed change only within the enrollment window; close new enrollment at the end of week3. Check lost orders, complaints, discount overrides and workload twice weekly. Preserve the holdout. Escalate any approved guardrail breach immediately rather than waiting for the final report. |
| 4 — review execution | Operations director + finance owner | Verify who received which price, exclusions, exceptions, demand and early contribution. Choose stop or continue within the already approved exposure cap. Label incomplete returns and freight provisional. Do not expand from an immature positive result. Schedule the mature readout using actual source availability. |

## Measure the choice the test can change

**Primary outcome:** net contribution per assigned eligible account over the same follow-up window, including accounts that buy nothing. Use net sales after posted credits, net COGS and all attributable outbound freight. Reporting only contribution per purchased order would hide lost demand. Account allocation is the unit of comparison; do not treat each invoice as an independent experiment.

**Guardrails:** order conversion or ordering rate, lost quotes/orders, customer retention, service complaints and operating workload. Agree numerical stop thresholds and an escalation owner in week1. Freeze those thresholds before seeing outcomes. If a customer contract or service commitment is breached, pause the affected action immediately and route it to the director.

**Readout record:** assignment, account eligibility, exposure dates, prices actually applied, order/return dates, net sales/COGS, matched freight, unmatched freight, complaint and lost-order reason. Finance owns definitions and reconciliation; the commercial lead owns execution; the director owns stop/continue/expand decisions. One person may cover several roles if authority and capacity permit.

## Week four is not automatically the outcome date

Enrollment ends on day21. A **proposed** 28-day observation window makes the last account mature on day49, not day28. The final usable readout is the latest of day49, the relevant return-adjustment cutoff and the ledger/freight finalization date. Week1 must confirm those cutoffs from the actual policies; the supplied exports do not specify them. Four weeks of operations is not a calendar accounting month.

At day28, stop any harmful action and report the evidence available. A later readout is a separate scheduled analysis proposal, not permission to keep changing prices after the approved test window. Finance may continue collecting authorized records for already enrolled accounts.

## Decision rule

Expand only when the mature comparison supports the prespecified worthwhile contribution gain, guardrails hold, source totals reconcile and uncertainty is small enough for the approved business risk. If sample size is insufficient, report the effect as unresolved and keep the approved fallback; a positive point estimate is not a win. If assignment was not random, disclose selection and use the result as a bounded operational comparison rather than a causal elasticity estimate.

Record the actual result, approved decision, execution status and next owner in one line of the decision log. Until that readout exists, the only completed result is this proposed plan.
