All money columns are million VND. order_id identifies one order; repeated identical exports are the same record. Refunds reduce gross revenue in the order period and join by order_id. cogs_net already incorporates recovered cost from returned goods. Net revenue = gross revenue minus refunds. Gross profit = net revenue minus cogs_net. After-ad contribution = gross profit minus advertising spend; other fixed, payment and fulfilment costs are not supplied, so this is not net profit. Monthly ads are summed once by period/channel, not attached once per order. No randomized or causal ad attribution is supplied. Next-month ad ceiling is 165 million VND. A proposed diagnostic can shift at most 20 million within that ceiling and run for 14 days; no changes are executed in this exercise.
