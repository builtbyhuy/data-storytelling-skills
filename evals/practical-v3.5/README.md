Extract one complete package, open `report.html` and follow its unchanged original README. Each retained source-based recipe was separately executed from a relocated copy and reproduced its declared outputs. These are offline HTML/text/vector artifacts, not enlarged screenshots. They need no server to view; an available Python runtime is needed only to rebuild. Forms/drafts simulate records and send nothing externally.

**Environment-specific optional QA is retained, not disguised as a portable recipe.** The K-Dense package includes two original browser-inspection scripts (`inspect-detail.mjs`, `verify-render.mjs`) that refer to the author's existing local runtime. They are inspection receipts, not dependencies of `build.py` or of the report. Use the documented standard-library Python build to reproduce the delivered content; use your own available browser tooling for additional checks. None of the other four complete practical packages contains a personal absolute path.

The [publication receipt](PUBLICATION.json) binds every archive and member to the original seal. Each package keeps UCI's original Wine Quality data, dictionary and CC BY 4.0 attribution: Cortez, Cerdeira, Almeida, Matos and Reis (2009), [UCI DOI 10.24432/C56S3T](https://doi.org/10.24432/C56S3T). The threshold and 40-record staffing scenario are hypothetical exercises. No real wine review, launch, approval, human preference study or commercial outcome occurred.

Author self-reviews and screenshots remain unchanged when present; they are not substituted for independent results. Disclosed method credits may reveal a condition. Five methods share a source, model family and installed catalog; they are not five independent datasets or an isolated causal experiment.
