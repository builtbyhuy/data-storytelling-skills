## Operational qualifications

Priority frameworks guide judgment; conflicting percentage/count thresholds in the source are not acceptance rules. Novelty, an Aha, monetary stakes, a fixed number of insights, short word counts, exact opening times and a fixed rollout duration are optional presentation choices. Statistical summaries must answer the chosen estimand; a mean/median gap does not select it. Sampling is legitimate when its scope and uncertainty are disclosed.

Source case periods, financial ratios, correlation-to-business-return conversions and outcome claims are not established facts about a new task. Use the underlying diagnostic questions with actual supplied data. Backward argument design follows analysis and retains disconfirming evidence. Ordinary magnitude bars use zero; labeled interval/change encodings require their own honest interpretation. Palette recipes do not certify accessibility.

AI stages and organizational roles describe responsibilities, not required agents, hires, warehouse maturity, services or guaranteed time savings. A source/snapshot, stable meanings and proportionate verification can support bounded local work. The bibliography is further reading, not a claim that the suite inspected every cited book.
