# Design an argument around an actual decision

## Brief and reader

Use TOSCAR to resolve material context, not to force six questions: Trouble(problem/why now), Owner(decision maker), Success(outcome and observation window), Constraints(budget/data/authority/practical limits), Actors(input/execution/affected roles), Reference(prior attempts and usable learning). Infer supplied facts; label reversible assumptions; ask only for missing information that changes consequential correctness.

Match reader, decision and data grain. Leadership may need a choice and tradeoffs, managers may need segment contributions and resource allocation, operators may need eligible cases and exceptions. These are starting points, not role stereotypes. Preserve needed drill-down and meaningful caveats for every audience. A layered artifact can serve compatible readers; genuinely different permissions or decisions can need different outputs.

## Evidence before rhetoric

Exploration considers patterns; explanation chooses what this reader needs. Maintain enough working evidence to revise the argument. Select the takeaway from evidence, then work backward to an opening, support and resolution. A familiar important risk or mixed finding can be useful; neither surprise nor monetary impact is mandatory.

An optional arc is Setting/Hook -> Rising evidence -> Aha/main takeaway -> Resolution. The hook can be an honest question or consequential contrast; do not fabricate a dramatic event or delay an urgent decision for suspense. In a short memo, put the recommendation first. Outline before visual polish where helpful, without producing an unnecessary planning document.

Use Eliminate -> Narrow -> Direct to test the argument: which alternative is actually ruled out, where the gap is localized, and which evidence supports the recommended direction. A segment contrast may narrow the investigation without proving cause. State uncertainty at the relevant link. Include findings in the main argument, accessible evidence, or an explicitly unresolved list according to their decision relevance; do not discard contradictory results to fit a fixed number of insights.

Translate Technical -> Business -> Action without changing the proposition. A coefficient remains an association unless design supports stronger inference. Financial stakes require compatible bases, periods and calculations. Retention loss does not automatically equal recoverable ARR, and advertising revenue return does not equal incremental profit ROI. Time, reliability, service quality and safety may be the relevant stakes.

## Resolution and medium

4D connects Define problem -> Desired outcome -> Do/actions -> Data to measure. Include a proposed or confirmed accountable role, feasible window, dependencies, tradeoffs, costs and stop/reversal rule where relevant. An outcome target is not a forecast. With sufficient evidence choose a concrete action; otherwise choose a specific diagnostic/experiment and say which result changes the decision.

Useful editing prompts are opening, context, finding, impact, proposal and next step. They are not six mandatory paragraphs. An email needs a descriptive subject and a concise decision-ready body; an alert needs scoped change/threshold, impact, owner and action; a deck needs evidence-bearing takeaway slides and accessible support; a memo needs the choice, reasons, limitations and next step. Respect the user's requested length and language, without imposing universal timing/word/slide quotas.

Before adding another table or framework, ask which reader action it enables. Put the preferred option, strongest relevant alternative, decisive tradeoff and condition that would change the choice close together. Separate an operational check-in from an outcome readout when their timing differs. Keep supporting reconciliations accessible without making the reader repeat the analysis to find the decision.

## Working canvas

For a substantial story, capture decision/candidate hypotheses, audience, stakes, honest opening, support, main finding, action, measure, risk/rollback and portable message. Fill known context and mark unknowns; do not prewrite an expected Aha as the answer. Reuse this canvas only when it helps execution, not as an eligibility gate for a small request.

## Acceptance

The reader can identify the decision and understand why the evidence supports it. Material claims trace to facts, decisive figures agree across all outputs, the strongest contradictory evidence remains visible, and recommendation/authority/measurement fit the brief. Verify actual rendered output for visual formats. Do not claim adoption, approval, realized value or professional certification from the artifact alone.
